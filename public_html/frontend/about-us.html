Our Story

Veiron Auto was founded in 2012 with the desire to offer mobility services at the highest standards for customers in Satu Mare, Maramureș, Cluj, Bihor and neighboring regions.

We have specialized in both fast and flexible car rentals and complete assistance for identifying and resolving car damages.

Our mission is to make travel and solving any car problem as simple, fast and stress-free as possible for every customer.

What Sets Us Apart

Experience & Reliability

We have been on the market for over 12 years and have helped thousands of clients, whether they came on vacation, business trip or went through unpleasant traffic events.

Complete Service

We provide 24/7 car rentals, delivery/pickup at any address in Satu Mare, Maramureș, Cluj, Oradea, Debrecen and Budapest, as well as permanent roadside assistance.

Free Damage Consultation

We fully handle the damage file, collaborate with all insurance companies and offer replacement car and free towing, at no cost to the damaged customer.

Transparency & Fairness

Minimal formalities, permanent communication and clear prices, adapted to everyone's needs.
